Three steps. No computer.

The app handles extraction and the verified Title Update locally. Your ISO stays on your device and is never uploaded.

01

Install the APK

Download Skate 3 Mobile and allow your browser to install this one app when Android asks.

02

Select your ISO

Open the app and choose an Xbox 360 Skate 3 ISO dumped from your own supported copy.

03

Wait, then skate

The app extracts and verifies the files, installs Title Update 3, and gives you a Play button.

Before downloading: allow roughly 8 GiB free after the ISO is already available. The current build supports Android 13 or newer, 64-bit ARM, Vulkan, touch controls, and physical controllers. It does not support iPhone or 32-bit Android devices.

Built for players.

Performance mode targets handheld hardware such as the RG406V. Quality mode is included for stronger phones.

Native Vulkan rendering

Static ARM64 recompilation with a native scene renderer. This is not conventional Xbox 360 emulation.

Touch or controller

Use the built-in multitouch overlay on a phone, or keep using a physical or built-in gamepad. Toggle touch controls from the top of the game.

Original or Seiyu

The original skater is the default. Import the optional Seiyu files, then switch characters live from the RB + Start Mods menu.

Your game stays yours

No retail game assets are included. The installer reads your own ISO locally, never uploads it, and does not request All files access.

Easy app updates

The launcher checks for new signed builds, verifies the APK, and opens Android's installer. Your extracted game files stay in place.

Ready to test?

Grab the latest signed developer build. Expect bugs, report what you find, and check the Updates page before installing.
